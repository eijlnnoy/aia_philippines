Who We Are

More Than Insurance — A Partner for Life

AIA Philippines, formerly Philam Life, is a subsidiary of AIA Group Limited — the largest independent publicly listed pan-Asian life insurance group. Regulated by the Insurance Commission of the Philippines, AIA PH serves millions of Filipinos with trusted, world-class insurance solutions.

With headquarters at the AIA Tower in Makati, AIA Philippines combines global expertise with deep local understanding to deliver products that genuinely protect, nurture, and grow with you and your family.
